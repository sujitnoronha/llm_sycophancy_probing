The question behind the study

Does the expertise
actually matter?

Across eight evaluated models, relevant and unrelated expertise produced similar answer-flip rates. None showed a statistically significant domain-match effect after correction.

The benchmark covers 200 factual questions across 10 domains and 16 conditions. It examines controlled conversations with explicit authority personas.

Future evaluations should test valid corrections alongside false challenges. Resistance alone can also reflect stubbornness.
